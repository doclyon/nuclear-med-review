Chou TH, Nabavinia M, Rimmerman ET, Mansfield C et al.
Center for Regenerative Medicine, The Research Institute at Nationwide Children's Hospital, 575 Children's Crossroad, Columbus, OH, WB413343215, USA.
Contexte
